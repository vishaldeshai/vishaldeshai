ResuFlex – resume builder with resizable split sections

ResuFlex is a free online resume builder. Split any section side by side or stacked, drag the dividers to resize, add labels, paragraphs and bullet lists with the + button, and format text with bullets, alignment, fonts and sizes. Export to PDF or DOCX. No sign-up; your resume is saved in your browser.

How it works

  1. Click a section on the resume page to select it.
  2. Split it side by side or stacked, then drag the divider to resize.
  3. Click + inside a section to add a label, paragraph or bullet list.
  4. Select text to apply bullets, alignment, fonts and sizes.
  5. Export to PDF or DOCX.

Frequently asked questions

How do I add a new text block inside a resume section?

Hover over or select a section and click the + button in its top-right corner. Choose Label, Paragraph or Bullet list and the new block is added at the end of that section.

How do I apply bullets, alignment or fonts to resume text?

Click into any text on the resume. A formatting toolbar appears with font, size, bold, italic, underline, bulleted list, numbered list and left, center, right and justify alignment.

Can I resize resume sections?

Yes. Drag any divider between two sections. With Snap on, dividers snap to 25%, 33%, 50%, 66% and 75%. You can also focus a divider and use the arrow keys, or apply a ratio preset from the Presets menu.

What formats can I export my resume to?

PDF, through your browser's print dialog, and DOCX, an editable Word document where side-by-side sections become table columns.

Is ResuFlex free and where is my resume saved?

ResuFlex is free with no sign-up. Your resume is saved automatically in your browser on this device and is not uploaded anywhere.
