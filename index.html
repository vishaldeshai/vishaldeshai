How to build a resume with split sections

Pick a layout, split and resize sections, add text with the + button, format it, then export. Most resumes take about ten minutes.

  1. Pick a layout presetClassic, Single column or Sidebar, from the Presets menu.
  2. Split and resizeSelect a section, split it side by side or top to bottom, then drag the divider.
  3. Add text with +Heading, label, paragraph, bullet list, label + date or small print.
  4. Format the textSelect text for bullets, numbering, alignment, font, size and color.
  5. ExportPDF at A4 through the print dialog, or an editable Word file.

Frequently asked questions

How do I add a new text block inside a resume section?

Hover or select the section and click the + button in its bottom-right corner. Choose Heading, Label, Paragraph, Bullet list, Label + date or Small print. The new block appears at the end of the section, ready to type in.

How do I apply bullets, alignment or a different font to resume text?

Select the text or place your cursor in a paragraph. A formatting toolbar appears above it with font, size, bold, italic, underline, bulleted list, numbered list, four alignment options, text color and clear formatting.

Can I resize resume sections?

Yes. Drag any divider between two sections. With Snap turned on, sizes move in 5% steps. You can also focus a divider with Tab and use the arrow keys, holding Shift for larger steps.

What file formats can I export my resume to?

PDF uses your browser's print dialog at A4 size, so choose Save as PDF there. Word downloads a .doc file that opens in Microsoft Word, Google Docs and LibreOffice, with side-by-side sections kept as table columns.

Is ResuFlex free and where is my resume stored?

ResuFlex is free and needs no account. Your resume is saved automatically in your own browser's local storage on this device and is not uploaded to a server.

Are split-column resumes ATS friendly?

Most applicant tracking systems read the text of a PDF in source order. ResuFlex keeps text as real, selectable text and orders it section by section, left to right and top to bottom. For the strictest systems, use the Single column preset.
